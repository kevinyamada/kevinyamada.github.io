WNBA Shot Atlas

I mapped 38,535 regular-season attempts from the 2025 WNBA season and compared spatial and Bayesian make-probability models. The app includes posterior uncertainty and an event-based possession-value prototype, with source checks and limitations documented.

Python · JavaScript · Spatial statistics

Canadian Household Finance

I audited what public Canadian saving figures can tell us about readiness to buy a home. The project separates reported net saving from deposit cash, checks annual totals, and applies price-tier down-payment rules. Explore a provincial housing-need map, purchase-cash scenarios, and a downloadable Power BI report.

Python · SQL · Power BI · Data visualization
